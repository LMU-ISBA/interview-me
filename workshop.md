# Let AI interview you first: the prompts

From the Center for Faculty Development session at Loyola Marymount
University, November 6, 2026.

## Before you start

1. Open a new chat in a new tab, and leave your first answer open in the
   other tab.
2. If your tool lets you choose, pick its strongest model and turn thinking
   up. The unknowns are all ahead of you.
3. Paste prompt 1 and put your idea in the blank.

Answer out loud if you like. Most AI tools have a microphone button next to
the message box, and your laptop has dictation built in. When it gives you
choices, pick one and say why. If none fits, say your own.

## Prompt 1: three questions

```text
Help me think through an idea: ___. Don't give a solution yet. Ask three
questions, one at a time, with multiple-choice options. Say in a
sentence why you're asking each question.
```

## Prompt 2: blind spot pass

```text
Based on my answers, do a blind spot pass. What are my unknown unknowns?
List five in rank order, one line each.
```

## Prompt 3: the spec

```text
Write a one-page spec: the goal, the constraints, and what good looks
like. Mark what you're guessing, and end with one next step. Then ask
what I'd update.
```

Fix at least one guess before you go on.

## Prompt 4: the visual

```text
Create a one-page visual of the spec that I can react to. Highlight your
guesses. Use short labels. If you can't show a picture, draw a text
diagram.
```

If it doesn't land, ask for two more:

```text
Show me two other ways to see this, as different from the first as you
can make them. Then ask me which one fits best and why.
```

## Later: pressure-test your spec

When you have a spec you're leaning toward, ask for five people who'd see it
differently, pick three, and compare where they split. The prompts are in
[prompts/perspectives-prompt.md](prompts/perspectives-prompt.md). Personas
from one model aren't independent opinions, so the value is having to answer
them.

## Use it as a Claude skill

If you use Claude, the same four steps are saved as a skill, so you don't
paste anything. Say "interview me first" in any new chat. The steps to add,
pause, remove, and change it are in the [README](README.md).
